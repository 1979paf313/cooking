## Follow-up candidates (not blockers)

- Nutrition calculation, brand-specific carbohydrate data, and finished batch weights have not been estimated here.
- Cook Mode still displays step narrative times as written; times do not multiply automatically when scaling.
- Some ingredients occur in more than one phase because they are prepared and used again. Cook Mode is designed to show them again when relevant.
- Whole eggs, rolls, loaves and cheese slices can create awkward fractional amounts at unusual scales; the app should continue to permit approximations.
- These are usable editorial recipe versions. Actual cooking feedback should replace assumptions as recipes are tested.
